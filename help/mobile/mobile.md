# Mobile

Marketo Mobile Engagement helps marketers deliver mobile experiences and respond to customer activity on mobile devices.

Marketo Mobile Engagement has two components:

1. Mobile SDK

    Integrate the Marketo Mobile SDK into your mobile application to deliver marketing content and gather analytics.

1. Mobile Marketing

    Use Marketo to define audiences, create notifications, launch targeted messaging campaigns, and view customer analytics.

After you integrate the Marketo SDK into your mobile application, you can send mobile push notifications and in-app messages from Marketo. To set up and deploy mobile messaging, see [Mobile Marketing](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/marketo/using/product-docs/mobile-marketing/admin/add-a-mobile-app) in the product documentation.
